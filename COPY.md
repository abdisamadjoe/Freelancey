# COPY.md

Rules for marketing and landing page copy.

- No em dashes.
- No fabricated claims or made-up numbers. ("Trusted by teams worldwide" not "Trusted by 50,000 teams" unless it's true)
- One idea per section. (Pricing section talks price only, not price + features + testimonial)
- Specific headlines that sell above the fold, not generic ones. ("Track expenses in 10 seconds" not "Welcome to the future of productivity")
- Benefit before feature. ("Find the right freelancer in minutes" not "Powered by AI matching")
- Proof goes next to the claim it backs. ("Saves 3 hours a week. ★★★★★ 'Cut my admin time in half' – Sarah K.")
- Handle the objection right before the CTA. ("No credit card needed. Start free trial.")
- CTA = verb + outcome. ("Start your free trial" not "Learn more")
- Write to one buyer, not everyone. ("Built for freelance designers" not "Perfect for businesses of all sizes")
- No "It's not X, it's Y" sentences. ("Saves you 3 hours a week" not "It's not just a tool, it's a movement")
